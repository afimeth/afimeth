Open the mechanism.

A design system for evidence-first engineering surfaces. The tip is the runnable surface. Everything below the waterline (fixtures, receipts, provenance, limits) gets equal design weight.

Principles

  • Evidence > claims
  • Receipts > screenshots
  • Explicit limits > polished certainty
  • One accent > many accents — signal amber marks the single thing that matters
  • Color encodes evidence class — never decoration
